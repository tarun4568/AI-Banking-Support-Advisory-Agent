CATEGORY 1: ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: How do I check my account balance?
A: You can check your balance through multiple channels:
   1. NovaTrust Mobile App → Dashboard → Account Summary.
   2. NetBanking → Accounts → Account Summary.
   3. NovaTrust ATM → Balance Enquiry (no charge).
   4. SMS: Type BAL <last 4 digits of account> and send to 56161.
   5. Missed call: 1800-NOVA-BAL (1800-6682-225) from registered mobile.
   6. Passbook update at any branch or passbook kiosk.

Q: How do I update my registered mobile number?
A: Visit your home branch with Aadhaar and PAN card. Submit a mobile number
   update form. Change is effective within 2 business days. For Premium account
   holders, the relationship manager can process this within 24 hours.
   Note: Mobile number cannot be updated via app or NetBanking for security reasons.

Q: How do I reset my NetBanking password?
A: Option 1 (Online): Go to www.novatrust.in → NetBanking Login → Forgot Password.
   Enter customer ID, date of birth, and registered mobile OTP.
   Option 2 (Branch): Visit any branch with photo ID. Password reset completed instantly.
   Locked accounts (after 3 wrong attempts) are automatically unlocked after 24 hours
   or immediately upon branch visit.

Q: Can I have multiple accounts at NovaTrust?
A: Yes. Customers can hold:
   • One primary savings account.
   • Additional savings accounts for specific purposes (e.g., goals, kids).
   • Multiple FDs and RDs.
   • One current account (per business entity).
   Note: Multiple savings accounts must each maintain minimum balance separately.

Q: How do I add a joint holder to my account?
A: Visit your home branch with both account holders' KYC documents.
   Fill out Form JP-3 (Joint Account Addition).
   Processing time: 3–5 business days.
   Joint holder types: "Either or Survivor" (most common) or "Jointly" (both signatures needed).

Q: What happens if I miss the minimum balance requirement?
A: A non-maintenance charge (NMC) of ₹200 + GST per quarter is levied.
   You receive an SMS notification 7 days before the quarter end if balance is below minimum.
   First-time NMC may be reversed on customer request (one-time goodwill waiver per account).

Q: What can I do on the NovaTrust Mobile App?

   • Fixed Deposit opening and closure.

Q: Is internet banking safe? What security measures does NovaTrust have?
A: NovaTrust NetBanking uses:
   • 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transmission.
   • Two-factor authentication (2FA) with OTP for every transaction.
   • Session timeout after 5 minutes of inactivity.
   • Device registration for trusted logins.
   • Transaction alerts via SMS and email for every debit/credit.
   • Cooling period of 4 hours for new payee registration before first transfer.
   Remember: NovaTrust staff will NEVER ask for your OTP, PIN, or password.

Q: How do I activate UPI on my NovaTrust account?
A: Option 1 – NovaTrust App: Download → Register → Set UPI PIN using debit card details.
   Your VPA (virtual payment address) will be yourID@novatrust.
   Option 2 – Third-party apps: Select NovaTrust bank during UPI setup;
   authenticate with debit card OTP sent to registered mobile.

Q: My transaction failed but money was debited. What should I do?
A: In most cases, the amount is automatically reversed within 24–48 hours.
   If not received after 48 hours:
   1. Raise a dispute via Mobile App → Transactions → Dispute.
   2. Call 1800-NOVA-123 with reference number.
   3. Visit any branch.
   The bank will investigate and reverse confirmed failed transactions within 5 business days.

Q: How do I check my loan eligibility?
A: Use the online eligibility calculator at www.novatrust.in/loan-eligibility.
   Enter your: income, existing EMIs, age, employment type.
   The calculator gives an indicative eligible loan amount.
   For accurate eligibility, approach any branch or relationship manager.
   Note: The calculator result is indicative only and does not constitute an approval.

Q: How is my EMI calculated?
A: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
   Where: P = Principal loan amount, R = Monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100),
   N = Loan tenure in months.
   Example: ₹10,00,000 home loan at 8.5% p.a. for 20 years:
   R = 8.5/12/100 = 0.007083; N = 240
   EMI ≈ ₹8,678 per month.
   Use our online calculator at www.novatrust.in/emi-calculator for your specific values.

Q: What is the difference between fixed and floating rate?
A: Fixed Rate: Interest rate stays the same throughout the loan tenure.
   Pros: Predictable EMI; protection against rate hikes.
   Cons: Higher initial rate; no benefit if rates fall; prepayment penalty.
   Floating Rate: Rate changes with RBI's repo rate changes (linked to EBLR).
   Pros: Generally lower than fixed; EMI reduces if rates fall.
   Cons: EMI may increase if rates rise; harder to plan long-term.

Q: Can I prepay my loan?
A: Home Loan (floating rate): Nil prepayment penalty (RBI mandate).
   Home Loan (fixed rate): 2% penalty if prepaying from own funds within 3 years.
   Personal Loan: Allowed after 6 EMIs; 2% part-prepayment charge; 3% foreclosure charge.
   Car Loan: Part-payment allowed after 12 EMIs; 2% charge; full foreclosure 3%.

Q: What is a CIBIL score and why does it matter for loans?
A: CIBIL score (Credit Score) is a 3-digit number (300–900) summarising your credit history.
   NovaTrust uses it to assess loan applications:
   750–900: Excellent – best rates, high approval likelihood.
   700–749: Good – generally approved with standard rates.
   650–699: Fair – may be approved with higher rates.
   Below 650: Poor – likely declined; improve first.
   Free CIBIL check: www.cibil.com (once per year free; ₹550 for additional checks).
   Improving score: Pay EMIs on time, reduce credit card utilisation, avoid multiple loan applications.

Q: Can I break my Fixed Deposit early?
A: Yes. Premature closure is allowed with a 1% penalty on the applicable rate.
   Example: If you booked FD at 7% for 2 years but break it at 1 year,
   the applicable rate for 1 year is 6.5%, and you get 6.5% – 1% = 5.5%.
   Tax-Saver FD (80C): Premature closure NOT allowed in first 5 years.
   FD linked as loan collateral: Cannot break until loan is cleared.

Q: How is FD interest taxed?
A: FD interest is taxable as "Income from Other Sources" at your income tax slab rate.
   TDS is deducted at source at 10% if interest exceeds ₹40,000 per year.
   (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
   To avoid TDS: Submit Form 15G (non-senior) or 15H (senior citizen) declaring
   that your total income is below taxable limit.
   Please consult a tax advisor for advice specific to your tax situation.

Q: What is the DICGC insurance cover on deposits?
A: The Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) insures bank deposits.
   Coverage: Up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (including principal and interest).
   This covers savings, FD, RD, and current accounts.
   NovaTrust Bank is insured under DICGC.

Q: How do I enable international usage on my debit card?
A: International usage is disabled by default on all debit cards.
   Enable via:
   1. NovaTrust App → Cards → International Usage → Enable.
   2. NetBanking → Cards → Manage Card → Enable International.
   3. Call 1800-NOVA-123.
   4. Branch visit.
   Enable only for the duration of travel and disable on return for safety.

Q: What is the forex markup on NovaTrust cards?
A: Foreign currency transactions attract:
   • RuPay Classic Debit: 3.5% markup.
   • Visa Platinum Debit: 2.0% markup.
   • RuPay Classic Credit: 3.0% markup.
   • Visa Gold Credit: 2.0% markup.
   • Visa Platinum Credit: 1.5% markup.
   Additionally, VISA/Mastercard/RuPay charges a 1% cross-border fee.

Q: How do I stop payment on a cheque?
A: To stop a cheque payment:
   1. Mobile App → Services → Stop Cheque Payment.
   2. NetBanking → Services → Cheque Services → Stop Payment.
   3. Call 1800-NOVA-123 (before cheque is presented).
   4. Visit branch with written request.
   Fee: ₹100 + GST per cheque.
   Note: A cheque that has already been encashed cannot be stopped.
