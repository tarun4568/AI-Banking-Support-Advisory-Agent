SECTION 1: NOVATRUST MOBILE APP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

App Name     : NovaTrust Bank – Mobile Banking
Availability : iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play)
Current Version: 6.8.2 (April 2026)
Languages    : English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati

Key Features:
1. Account Management
   - Real-time balance & statements (up to 2 years).
   - Set account nicknames for easy identification.
   - Download statements in PDF or Excel format.

2. Payments & Transfers
   - UPI payments (NovaTrust BHIM UPI).
   - IMPS: Instant 24/7 transfers.
   - NEFT/RTGS: Scheduled or immediate.
   - Bill payment: Electricity, water, gas, broadband, insurance.
   - Mobile/DTH recharge.
   - FASTag recharge.
   - NovaTrust Scan & Pay (QR-based).

3. Investments
   - Open Fixed Deposit (from ₹10,000).
   - Open Recurring Deposit (from ₹500/month).
   - Tax-Saver FD (Section 80C).

4. Loans
   - View loan account details and EMI schedule.
   - Prepayment requests (with forecast of savings).
   - Apply for pre-approved personal loan.

5. Card Services
   - Block/unblock debit and credit cards.
   - Change ATM PIN (using OTP authentication).
   - Set daily transaction limits.
   - Enable/disable international usage.
   - Enable/disable contactless payments.
   - Request physical credit card upgrade.

6. Locker Information (where applicable)
   - View locker status and renewal due date.

7. Service Requests (raised inside app, tracked with CRN):
   - Cheque book request.
   - Address change request (KYC re-verification required).
   - Nomination update.
   - Complaint registration.

Security Features on App:
   - Biometric (fingerprint / Face ID) login.
   - 6-digit MPIN as alternative.
   - Device binding: App can only be used on one registered device.
   - Session auto-logout: After 5 minutes of inactivity.
   - Screenshot disabled for security screens.

Services available only at branch:
1. Account opening, closure, and type conversion.
2. KYC update (address, name change, mobile if app fails).
3. Physical cheque deposit and cash deposit above app limits.
4. Demand Draft (DD) issuance.
5. Safe Deposit Locker: Allotment, nomination, and access.
6. Loan documentation submission and disbursement.
7. Signature update.
8. Senior citizen priority banking services.

Safe Deposit Lockers:
   - Available at 1,200+ branches.
   - Sizes: Small (₹1,500–₹2,500/year), Medium (₹3,000–₹4,500/year),
     Large (₹5,000–₹8,000/year), Extra Large (on request).
   - Key-operated with dual-key system (bank retains one key).
   - CCTV monitored vault access.
   - Access hours: Branch working hours only.
   - Waiting list: Register at branch if no locker currently available.

SECTION 4: NOVATRUST INSURANCE PRODUCTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NovaTrust distributes (does not underwrite) insurance products as a corporate agent.

4.1 Life Insurance
   Partner: NovaTrust Life Insurance Co. Ltd. (NovaTrust holds 25% stake).
   Products offered:
   • Term Insurance: Pure protection; ₹50 lakh–₹5 crore cover; online issuance.
   • Endowment Plan: Savings + protection; maturity benefit.
   • Unit-Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs): Market-linked returns + life cover.
   • Child Plan: Education corpus building.
   Important: These are insurance products, not bank deposits. Market risk applicable to ULIPs.

4.2 General Insurance
   Partner: NovaTrust General Insurance Ltd.
   Products:
   • Home Insurance: Structure and contents cover.
   • Motor Insurance: Comprehensive and third-party.
   • Health Insurance: Individual and floater plans.
   • Personal Accident Insurance.
   Important: Premiums vary. Coverage subject to policy terms and conditions.

SECTION 5: NOVATRUST INVESTMENT SERVICES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

NovaTrust distributes investment products as a SEBI-registered distributor.

5.1 Mutual Funds
   Platform: NovaTrust MF Marketplace (app + web).
   Minimum SIP: ₹500/month.
   Minimum Lumpsum: ₹5,000.
   AMC Partners: All 44 SEBI-registered AMCs available.
   KYC Requirement: Aadhaar-based eKYC via NovaTrust platform.
   Important: Mutual funds are subject to market risks. Read all scheme documents
   carefully before investing. Past returns do not guarantee future returns.

5.2 Demat & Trading Account
   Powered by: NovaTrust Securities Ltd. (SEBI registered broker).
   Open Demat: Via NovaTrust app (paperless, takes 3–5 business days).
   Brokerage: ₹15 per order (equity delivery); ₹20 per order (intraday, F&O).
   Features: Live market data, research reports, portfolio tracking.

5.3 National Pension System (NPS)
   NovaTrust is a Point of Presence (PoP) for NPS under PFRDA.
   Open NPS account online via NetBanking or at any branch.
   Tax benefit: Up to ₹2 lakh under Section 80C + ₹50,000 additional under 80CCD(1B).
