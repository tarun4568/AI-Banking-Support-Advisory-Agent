SECTION 1: ACCOUNT OPENING POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements
───────────────────────────────────────────
All individuals opening accounts at NovaTrust Bank must complete KYC as per
RBI guidelines. KYC is mandatory and non-negotiable.

Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) accepted:
  Identity Proof (any one):
  • Aadhaar Card (mandatory for eCKYC)
  • PAN Card
  • Passport
  • Voter ID Card
  • Driving Licence
  • NREGA Job Card

  Address Proof (any one if address differs from ID proof):
  • Utility bills (not older than 3 months)
  • Bank statement with address (not older than 3 months)
  • Rent agreement (registered)
  • Letter from employer on official letterhead

Additional Requirements for Minors (below 18):
  • Guardian's complete KYC documents.
  • Birth certificate of the minor.
  • School ID card (if available).

1.2 Account Opening Process
─────────────────────────────
Step 1: Visit any NovaTrust branch or apply online at www.novatrust.in.
Step 2: Fill out the account opening form (AOF) — available online or at branch.
Step 3: Submit self-attested KYC documents.
Step 4: Fund initial deposit (if minimum balance account).
Step 5: Account activated within 1 business day for in-person; 2–3 days for online.
Step 6: Debit card and cheque book dispatched within 7–10 working days.
Step 7: NetBanking credentials sent to registered email within 3 working days.

1.3 eCKYC (Aadhaar-based Paperless KYC)
─────────────────────────────────────────
NovaTrust offers eCKYC using Aadhaar OTP-based authentication.
This is the fastest way to open a savings account, completed in under 10 minutes.
Applicable for residents with Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
Account becomes fully operational after successful eCKYC.

1.4 Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Accounts
──────────────────────────────────────
NovaTrust offers:
  • NRE (Non-Resident External) Savings Account: Fully repatriable; interest tax-free in India.
  • NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) Savings Account: For income earned in India (rent, dividends).
  • FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) Deposits: Hold money in USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, SGD.
Additional documents: Passport with visa, overseas address proof, NRI declaration form.

SECTION 2: ATM & CARD POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Debit Card Usage Limits (Default, can be changed by customer)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Daily cash withdrawal (ATM): ₹25,000 (Classic) | ₹50,000 (Platinum).
  • Daily POS/online spending: ₹1,00,000 (Classic) | ₹3,00,000 (Platinum).
  • International usage: Disabled by default; enable via app or branch.

2.2 Lost or Stolen Card
────────────────────────
Immediately hotlist (block) card through:
  • NovaTrust Mobile App → Cards → Block Card.
  • NetBanking → Services → Card Management → Block.
  • Call 24/7 helpline: 1800-NOVA-123 (toll-free).
  • Visit any branch with ID proof.
Replacement card issued within 5–7 working days; express delivery available.
Zero liability on fraudulent transactions reported within 3 working days.
Partial liability (max ₹10,000) for transactions reported after 3 but within 7 days.

2.3 Credit Card Billing Cycle
───────────────────────────────
  • Billing date: Fixed as per card issue date (shown on statement).
  • Payment due date: 20 days after billing date.
  • Minimum amount due: 5% of outstanding or ₹500, whichever is higher.
  • Late payment charges: ₹100–₹1,300 depending on outstanding balance.

SECTION 3: FUND TRANSFER POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer)
───────────────────────────────────────────────
Timings: Available 24x7 (as per RBI circular, December 2019).
Settlement: In 30-minute batches; within 2 hours typically.
Customer-facing limits: ₹10 lakh per transaction via NetBanking (can be changed with RM).
Charges: Free online; ₹2–₹25 via branch depending on amount.

3.2 RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement)
────────────────────────────────────────
Minimum Amount: ₹2 lakh.
Timings: 24x7 (RBI circular CIR-2020-0070).
Settlement: Real-time (within minutes).
Charges: Free online; ₹25–₹50 via branch.

3.3 IMPS (Immediate Payment Service)
──────────────────────────────────────
Available: 24x7 including weekends and holidays.
Limit: Up to ₹5 lakh per transaction via mobile app/NetBanking.
Charges: ₹5–₹15 + GST depending on amount band.

3.4 UPI (Unified Payments Interface)
──────────────────────────────────────
NovaTrust VPA format: customerID@novatrust
Daily limit per transaction: ₹1 lakh (₹2 lakh for verified merchants).
Daily overall limit: ₹1 lakh (unless enhanced by bank).
Available on: NovaTrust Bhim UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm.
Dispute resolution: Raise UPI dispute via app within 30 days of transaction.

SECTION 4: SECURITY & FRAUD PREVENTION POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
──────────────────────────────────────
All NetBanking and mobile app transactions require:
  1. Login password / MPIN.
  2. OTP on registered mobile number.
Credit/debit card online transactions require 3D Secure OTP.
NovaTrust will NEVER ask for your password, PIN, OTP, or CVV via phone/email/chat.

4.2 Safe Banking Tips
───────────────────────
  • Never share OTP, PIN, or password with anyone — not even bank staff.
  • Verify the NovaTrust website URL: always https://www.novatrust.in.
  • Do not click links in unsolicited SMS/email claiming to be from NovaTrust.
  • Update registered mobile number promptly if it changes.
  • Enable transaction alerts via SMS and email.
  • Review bank statements monthly; report discrepancies immediately.

4.3 Fraud Reporting
─────────────────────
Report suspected fraud immediately:
  • 24/7 Fraud Hotline: 1800-NOVA-FRAUD (1800-6682-37283).
  • Cybercrime Portal: www.cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Nearest NovaTrust branch.
Response: Bank will block affected accounts and cards within 4 hours of verified report.

SECTION 5: COMPLAINT RESOLUTION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5.1 Grievance Redressal Process
──────────────────────────────────
Level 1 – Customer Service:
  Register complaint via: Mobile app / NetBanking / Phone 1800-NOVA-123 / Branch.
  Resolution time: Within 5 business days for most issues.
  Complaint Reference Number (CRN): Provided immediately; use to track status.

Level 2 – Nodal Officer:
  If not resolved in 5 days or dissatisfied: Escalate to zonal nodal officer.
  Email: nodalofficer@novatrust.in | Response within 10 business days.

Level 3 – Principal Nodal Officer:
  For unresolved grievances: principal.nodal@novatrust.in.
  Response within 15 business days.

Level 4 – Banking Ombudsman (RBI):
  If not resolved within 30 days or solution unsatisfactory.
  File at: https://cms.rbi.org.in (free service).
  Powers: Can award compensation up to ₹20 lakh.

5.2 Common Complaint Types & TAT
──────────────────────────────────
  • Disputed ATM/POS transaction: 5–7 business days.
  • Disputed credit card charge: 7–10 business days.
  • Account debited but transfer failed: Credit within 1 business day.
  • Cheque clearing dispute: 24 hours after investigation.
  • KYC-related account freeze: 3–5 business days.

SECTION 6: ACCOUNT CLOSURE & DORMANCY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1 Account Closure
──────────────────────
Request account closure: Visit home branch with ID proof and original passbook/card.
Close within 12 months: No charge (for Classic accounts).
Close after 12 months: No charge.
Close within 14 days of opening: No charge.
Outstanding dues: Must be cleared before closure.
Standing instructions: Must be cancelled before closure request.

6.2 Dormant Account Policy
────────────────────────────
Account becomes dormant if no customer-initiated transaction in 12 months.
Account freezes (no debits allowed) if no transaction in 24 months.
Reactivation: Visit branch with KYC documents and a written reactivation request.
Unclaimed deposits: Transferred to RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund after 10 years.

SECTION 7: NOMINATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Nomination allows a designated person to receive account funds upon the
account holder's death, simplifying the legal process.

  • How to add: Form DA-1 available at branch or online portal.
  • One nominee per account (joint accounts can have different nominees).
  • Nominee can be changed or cancelled anytime by the account holder.
  • Nomination does not create legal title — nominee acts as trustee for legal heirs.
  • For FDs and lockers, separate nomination forms required.
