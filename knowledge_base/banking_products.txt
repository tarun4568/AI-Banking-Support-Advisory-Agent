1.1 Nova Classic Savings Account
─────────────────────────────────
Purpose        : For everyday personal savings and transactions.
Minimum Balance: ₹5,000 (urban branches) | ₹2,000 (semi-urban) | ₹500 (rural)
Interest Rate  : 3.5% per annum on daily closing balance.
Free ATM Txns  : 5 per month from NovaTrust ATMs; 3 from other bank ATMs.
Debit Card     : RuPay Classic debit card, free for first year.
Cheque Book    : First 25 leaves free per year.
Eligibility    : Indian resident individual aged 18+, valid KYC documents.
SMS Alerts     : Free for all transactions.
Online Banking : Free access via NetBanking and NovaTrust Mobile App.
Passbook       : Available at any NovaTrust branch, free of charge.

Fees & Charges:
  • Non-maintenance of minimum balance: ₹200 + GST per quarter.
  • ATM cash withdrawal beyond free limit: ₹20 per transaction.
  • NEFT/RTGS via branch: ₹25–₹50 per transaction depending on amount.
  • Cheque dishonour: ₹500 per instance.

1.2 Nova Premium Savings Account
──────────────────────────────────
Purpose        : Premium account with enhanced benefits and higher interest.
Minimum Balance: ₹25,000 (quarterly average)
Interest Rate  : 4.0% per annum on daily closing balance.
Free ATM Txns  : Unlimited at NovaTrust ATMs; 5 per month at other banks.
Debit Card     : Visa Platinum debit card with travel insurance (₹5 lakh cover).
Locker Discount: 20% discount on first year locker rental.
Relationship Mgr: Dedicated relationship manager for accounts with QAB > ₹1 lakh.
International Txns: Foreign currency markup 2% (vs 3.5% Classic).
Priority Banking: Priority queue at all branches.

1.3 Nova Junior Savings Account
──────────────────────────────────
Purpose        : For children aged 0–17, operated jointly with parent/guardian.
Minimum Balance: Nil (zero balance account).
Interest Rate  : 4.5% per annum (special promotional rate).
Daily Withdrawal Limit: ₹2,000 (for minors to learn financial responsibility).
Debit Card     : RuPay Junior debit card with curated spending controls.
Converts to    : Nova Classic account automatically at age 18.
Eligibility    : Minor child; parent/guardian must be NovaTrust customer.

5.1 Nova Home Loan
────────────────────
Purpose        : Purchase, construction, renovation of residential property.
Loan Amount    : ₹5 lakh to ₹5 crore.
Tenure         : Up to 30 years.
Interest Rate  : 8.40%–9.50% p.a. (floating, linked to EBLR); 8.75%–9.75% (fixed).
LTV Ratio      :
  • Up to ₹30 lakh: 90% of property value.
  • ₹30 lakh–₹75 lakh: 80% of property value.
  • Above ₹75 lakh: 75% of property value.
Processing Fee : 0.5% of loan amount + GST (waived during festive campaigns).
Prepayment     : Nil for floating rate; 2% for fixed rate within 3 years.
EMI Calculation: Online EMI calculator at www.novatrust.in/home-loan-emi
Insurance      : Home loan protection insurance recommended but not mandatory.
Moratorium     : Up to 6 months for under-construction properties.

Eligibility (indicative):
  • Salaried: Age 21–60; minimum net salary ₹25,000/month; 2 years employment.
  • Self-employed: Age 21–65; minimum 3 years business vintage; ITR required.
  • Credit score: Minimum 700 CIBIL recommended.

Documents Required (salaried):
  • KYC: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport-size photos.
  • Income: Last 3 months' salary slips; Form 16; 6 months' bank statements.
  • Property: Sale deed, property tax receipts, approved building plan.

5.2 Nova Home Loan – Balance Transfer
──────────────────────────────────────
Transfer your existing home loan to NovaTrust.
Benefit        : Lower interest rate if your current rate is ≥ 9.25%.
Processing Fee : ₹5,000 flat + GST.
Eligibility    : Existing loan 6+ months old; no default history.
